A Letter For Your 20th Birthday

Written with all my heart · November 17, 2026

To: Lana Raynaud

Hiii nanaaaaa, happy 20 Years Old birthday!! Mwah mwah! 🧸🤍✨

Sitting here today, my heart feels so full, a little emotional, and honestly a bit sad because I’m not right there beside you in Poitiers to hold your hand and cuddle you and look into your eyes, and wish you a happy birthday in person. anywayyy this year i’m 23 and you’re finally turning 20yo today yayyyy hihih makes me smile so big, yet it makes me miss you so much it aches. I just want to pull you into the longest, tightest hug right now and never let go nana.

and again happy 20th birthday to my sweet autumn girl, my cozy girl, my cute girl with that effortless chic glow. anyway you look so pretty and put together on the outside, and yes, I love spoiling my cute shopaholic girl but Nana, I want to be completely honest with you from the bottom of my heart. I don’t love you for your looks, your body, or anything materialistic tbh. those things fade, and I’m just a simple guy who values what’s real. I love you because I truly see who you are on the inside. Your pure heart, your softness, your goofy side, the way you care, and the honest, gentle soul beneath everything that is what made me fall so deeply in love with you.

yk even on the days when distance feels heavy and I get a little sad wishing I could just teleport to your side, loving you makes me the happiest guy alive. nana you bring so much warmth, peace, and joy into my life just by being you. i promise to always be a gentle, loyal, patient, and honest man, and someone who holds you down through every high and low, listens to you, protects your heart, and loves you unconditionally, not for what we have, but for who we are together.

right now, my biggest dream is counting down the days until i move to my apartment in cambridge and i can finally close to nana and get on a flight straight to poitiers hhihihi. I picture it all the time like walking through your door, wrapping my arms around you, breathing you in, and just cuddling on the couch for hours. cooking together, laughing at silly things, keeping you warm, and making up for every single day we spent apart yayyyy and sleep and i take silly nana leak saliva from mouth when asleep and record when u snore.

and nana I’m always praying that you pass every exam with the best grades at Poitiers University and grow into the brilliant, compassionate lawyer I know you’re meant to be. While you chase your dreams in law and I work hard building my path as a businessman, know that we are walking side by side. I am endlessly proud of you, endlessly grateful for you, and missing you more than words could ever explain.

Happy 20th birthday, my love. Have the coziest, happiest day and save a million hugs and cuddles for me.

Mwah, always seeing inside of you and loving the real you,

Azzi Raasha 🤍
